Market Context
Scotland personal care market — Jan 2024 to Apr 2026

Two years of month-on-month data tells a consistent story: the market is contracting — fewer jobs posted, fewer jobseekers searching — but the ratio of candidates to available roles has held up well, meaning the talent pool remains proportionate. The bigger challenge is financial: market competition has driven costs significantly higher, making every hire more expensive to attract.

Takeaways & Recommendations
What the data tells us — and what to do next

Based on the Jan–Apr 2026 Scotland market data, three clear themes emerge. Below are the recommended actions tied directly to what the numbers are showing.

📍
Protect your dominant positions
Call-In Homecare holds the #1 rank in Edinburgh, East Lothian, Midlothian, North Lanarkshire and Inverclyde. These are high-value locations with strong jobseeker penetration. The risk is complacency — competitors like Northcare Scotland and Cera Care are active in the same areas.

PSJ Maintain budget priority in these locations to hold rank 1.
EBA Run Employer Branded Ads to build brand recall — the 28–31% of jobseekers browsing without a keyword are your biggest brand opportunity.
📈
Close the gap in competitive locations
In Glasgow City (rank 3), Renfrewshire (rank 5) and Dundee City (rank 5), Call-In Homecare is visible but not leading. These are large markets — Glasgow alone represents 39,000+ jobseekers. Closing the gap to #1 here would significantly increase overall candidate reach.

PSJ Premium placement to outbid current rank 1 advertisers and capture more of the browse and keyword traffic.
EBA Branded ads reinforce Call-In Homecare as the recognisable name in the care space as candidates scroll the feed.
🚀
Act on rising market costs before they compound
Investment per job has risen +84% since Jan 2024, cost per click +82%, and cost per apply start +52%. This is a market-wide trend driven by more employers competing for fewer jobs. Waiting means paying more later for the same results.

PSJ Locks in premium placement at current rates — delays increase cost to compete as more advertisers adopt premium.
EBA Branded impressions build an audience of passive candidates who return directly — reducing reliance on expensive keyword-driven clicks over time.
PSJ Premium Sponsored Jobs — Priority placement at the top of Indeed search results, above standard sponsored listings. Wins visibility in competitive bidding environments.| EBA Employer Branded Ads — Job listings displayed with Call-In Homecare's logo and brand. Turns browse traffic into brand recognition and differentiates listings in a crowded feed.
